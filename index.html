ABOUT IGCCST 2026

Connecting cryogenic science & technology.

The Indo-German Conference on Cryogenics brings together scientists, researchers, engineers, and industry experts working across the broad field of cryogenic science and engineering.

The conference aims to strengthen scientific collaboration between India and Germany while highlighting emerging applications of cryogenic technologies in energy, hydrogen, quantum technologies, superconductivity, and space research.

CONFERENCE THEMES

Explore the research landscape.

Emerging developments across cryogenic science, engineering, and next-generation applications.

01
CRYOGENIC SYSTEMS

Cooling & Liquefaction

Gas liquefaction cycles, cryocoolers, refrigeration technologies, and advanced heat transfer systems.

02
INFRASTRUCTURE

Cryogenic Liquid Storage & Distribution

Dewars, insulated transfer lines, boil-off gas management, cryo-pumping, and transport safety.

03
CLEAN ENERGY

Hydrogen & LNG Technologies

Liquid hydrogen production, LNG supply chains, clean fuel storage, and sustainable energy transitions.

04
ENGINEERING

Cryogenic Infrastructure

Large-scale cryogenic test facilities, instrumentation, process control, and industrial plants.

05
SPACE

Cryogenic Space Technologies

Upper-stage cryogenic propulsion, in-space fluid management, space telescopes, and thermal control.

06
ULTRALOW TEMPERATURE

Ultralow Temperature Refrigeration

Dilution refrigerators, Adiabatic Demagnetization Refrigerators, and sub-Kelvin cooling systems.

07
QUANTUM

Cryogenics for Quantum Technologies

Superconducting qubits, quantum sensors, cryogenic interconnects, and scalable quantum architectures.

08
MATERIALS

Superconductivity & Quantum Materials

High-temperature superconductors, magnetic confinement coils, power transmission, and emergent materials.
